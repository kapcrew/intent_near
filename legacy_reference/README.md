# Historical Solidity reference — NOT NEAR deployable

The `.sol` sources in `lineage_solidity/` were extracted from the provided `lineageProtocol.zip` solely to illustrate provenance and provide a comparison basis. They are not compiled, used, modified for NEAR, or included in the NEAR frontend. NEAR executes WASM contracts, not Solidity/EVM bytecode. The ported policy and review behaviors live in `contracts/agent_policy`, `contracts/property_registry`, and `contracts/property_cells`.

All prior `.env`, `secretFiles`, keys, other potentially credential-bearing files and `__MACOSX` contents were deliberately omitted. Source copyright and permissions remain with their original owner; this archive does not relicense the historical sources.
